DAY 2

Welcome to Day 2 of UNITY 2026'! Get ready for a celebration of confidence, talent, personality, and individuality as we witness the search for Mr. and Ms. AKWE 2026 and the LGBTQIA+ Ambassador. Let us come together to support the candidates and celebrate diversity, unity, and school spirit.

DAY 2 ACTIVITIES

Morning – Search for Mr. and Ms. AKWE 2026 Day 2 will feature the search for Mr. and Ms. AKWE 2026, where candidates will showcase their confidence, talent, personality, and individuality. The event will highlight the candidates’ skills and abilities while encouraging everyone to support and celebrate their fellow students. LGBTQIA+ Ambassador The celebration will also include the search for the LGBTQIA+ Ambassador, recognizing confidence, individuality, diversity, and self-expression. This activity promotes respect, acceptance, unity, and inclusivity within the school community.
